# EasyNotes92 — Privacy Policy

*Last updated: November 22, 2026*

EasyNotes92 is a free note-taking app for Windows made by Mohamed Iraqi Houssaini (publisher name: SG92).

## The short version

**EasyNotes92 does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data.** It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it never sends anything over the internet.

## What stays on your computer

- **Your notes** are saved only where you choose to save them, as normal `.txt` / `.rtf` files.
- **App settings and your open tabs** (theme, font size, recent files, unsaved tabs, so they come back next time) are saved only on your PC, in `%APPDATA%\EasyNotes92`.
- **Autocomplete suggestions** are built from your own notes, on your PC. They are never uploaded anywhere.

Uninstalling the app and deleting the `%APPDATA%\EasyNotes92` folder removes everything the app stored.

## Network use

EasyNotes92 does not connect to the internet. The only network feature is a local connection on your own computer (`127.0.0.1`). When you open the app a second time, this connection hands your files to the window that is already open instead of starting a second copy. Nothing leaves your PC.

## Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.
